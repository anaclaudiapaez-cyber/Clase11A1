Grammar focus · 10 min

Can / can't

We use can and can't for abilities and for possibilities.

AffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / you / he / she / we / they can swim.I / you / he / she / we / they can't swim.Can you swim? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
💪

Ability

  • She can speak three languages.
✅

Possibility

  • Students can travel by bus with a discount.
🚫

Not possible

  • You can't use your phone in the exam.
  • We can't go hiking today: it's stormy.
!

Be careful

  • The verb never changes: he can swim (not "cans" or "can swims").

Reading · 15 min

Study in Chile

An exchange programme leaflet and two students' opinions.

EXCHANGE PROGRAMME — University of Valparaíso, Chile

Students from our university can apply twice a year, in March and in August. The programme lasts one semester. Students can choose between two cities: Valparaíso and Santiago.

The International Office helps students with the papers and the student card. With the student card, students can use the library, the computer lab and the sports centre, and they can get a discount on buses and trains. There is free Wi-Fi in all the buildings.

Weather: In summer, it's sunny and hot. In winter, it's cold, windy and often rainy. Bring a warm jacket!

Ana, 22, Geography student: Hi! I'm Ana. I'm an exchange student in Valparaíso. I love hiking, so every weekend I go to the hills near the city. I usually travel by bus because it's cheap and I can see the landscape. I don't like planes: they're expensive and I hate waiting at the airport. The weather here is great in summer, but in winter it's windy.

Marco, 24, Systems Engineering student: My name is Marco and I study in Santiago. I like museums and concerts, and I can speak Spanish, English and a little Italian. I prefer the train to the bus because it's faster. My favourite place is the university library: it's quiet and the Wi-Fi is very good. I don't like the cold, so I can't go to the mountains in winter!

A review for the Exchange Blog

I recommend Bariloche for your exchange holidays. In winter it's cold and snowy, so you can go skiing. I love hiking there because the views are beautiful. You can travel by bus, but it's slow, so I prefer the plane. I don't like the cold wind, but I enjoy drinking hot chocolate in the cafés. In my opinion, it's expensive but fantastic!

Teacher note

The review recycles likes and preferences, can/can't, because, weather and transport. Since students have not studied the past tense yet, travel experiences are shared as opinions, recommendations and habits in the present.
